# Release Notes

Version v2.2.0 — April 1, 2026

## Stab Performance Section

New "Stab Performance" section that shows per-player Stability generation across fights. You can drill into any fight to see 5-second generation buckets with party overlays — deaths, distance from tag, and per-member stab uptime stacks are all layered on top so you can spot exactly when and why stab dropped.

Players are sorted by squad generation output, and you can filter by name, account, or profession. The fight-level chart highlights the selected player against the fight's top performer for quick comparison.
